# My Chocolate Blondies recipe

Hey everyone, some people have been asking for my cookie recipe for a while.
I don't have anywhere else I could post it and I'm a little tired of sending
messages directly so here it is.

This recipe makes 72 cookies, aproximately in 4x4cm squares if using a 30x40cm
oven tray.

# Ingredients

- 250g white sugar
- 200g brown/yellow sugar
- 280g butter
- 3    eggs
- 1tbs vanilla extract
- 315g wheat flour
- 1ts  baking powder
- 1ts  table salt
- 300g chocolate bars or chocolate chips (as much as you would like)

## Note on the butter

There's a few ways to add butter to this recipe. If you want to have the
easiest fastest cookies, just add the cold or room-temperature butter to the
mix. This will create a cookie that is less chewy and will stick to the top of
your mouth.

If you want the cookies to be chewy and very gooey, melt the butter in a pan on
a stove and bring to a boil then turn off the stove. Wait 10 minutes to cool a
little before adding to the mix. This is the easiest method to use when you
don't have a stand mixer.

If you want cookies that are airy and melt in your mouth, melt the butter in a
pan on a stove, sift all the milk fats with a spoon when it stars boiling,
for around 2 or 3 minutes before turning it off. Wait 10 minutes to cool a
little, then put in in the fridge to cool for 1 hour or until the butter is
hard and yellow. After that you can add the cold butter to the mix.

# Equipment

- Stand mixer with paddle attachment OR a very good right arm, a spatula and
a strong will
- Shallow oven baking pan (it has to be shallow, if you use a deep one you
will have a hard time taking the cookies out)
- Spatula
- Sieve or strainer (small enough for flour)
- Oven (180°C)
- Another bowl (big enough to fit a head of cabbage for example)

# Process

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C;
2. Add both sugars to the mixing bowl and mix them evenly;
3. Add butter and mix till a homogeneous cream/paste is made;
4. Add 1 egg and mix until even;
5. Repeat step 4 until all eggs have been added;
6. Add 1 tablespoon of vanilla extract and mix evenly;
7. In a separate bowl, mix flour, 1 teaspoon of baking powder and 1 teaspoon of
salt;
8. Add the dry mix into the wet mix using a sieve to make sure no clumps of
flour pass into the mix;
9. Mix until even;
10. On a cutting board, cut your chocolate bars into pieces as big as the nail
on your index finger, if you use chocolate chips just add them in;
11. Line a baking tray with parchment paper, edge to edge;
12. Add the mix to the baking tray and spread evenly (the patience required
for this step is astounding, spreading evenly takes time and effort. I use the
spatula and a fork to help);
13. Bake in the oven for 35 minutes;
14. Remove from the oven and let cool for around 20 minutes;
15. You should be able to slide the parchment paper along with the giant
blondie out with no issues. However, sometimes the cookie will break when
you're taking it out. To remedy this issue, you can pray to the gods a few
hours before sleep and maybe one day they will listen to your request while
scrolling god twitter in the god toilet;
16. Cut into squares. I don't use a ruler because I kinda remember the size by
now but if you want to have a perfect amount, you should measure and cut in
perfect squares or longer rectangles.
